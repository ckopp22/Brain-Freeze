HOW TO PLAY

  1. 🎛️Pick your players and settings.
  2. 📱Pass the device. Each player gets one card per turn.
  3. ⏱️Beat the clock by answering out loud.
  4. 🔄Flip the card. The group decides: NAILED IT (+100) or FROZE (0).
  5. 🏆First to 1,000 points (10 cards) wins!

💬 "Name 5…" cards: you need all of them to count, unless the card says otherwise.
